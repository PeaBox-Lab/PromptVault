# PromptVault

**1,000 detailed, reusable AI prompt templates in Markdown across 75 categories.**

**750 reusable AI prompt templates in Markdown, spanning business functions, industries, sectors, and advanced research methods.**

PromptVault is repository-first: Markdown is the source of truth, every prompt has its own page, every category has an index, and `prompts.json` is the machine-readable catalog.

## Library

- **750 prompt pages**
- **50 categories** spanning professional functions, sectors, industries, and research methods
- Reusable `{{placeholder}}` inputs
- High-stakes templates explicitly framed as decision support with human review

## Prompt page standard

Each page includes a stable ID, purpose, copy-ready prompt, placeholders, usage guidance, and quality checks.

## Safety and professional use

Prompts relating to health, law, finance, cybersecurity, engineering, or other high-stakes areas are designed for research and decision support, not autonomous professional judgment. Outputs should be validated against authoritative evidence and reviewed by appropriately qualified people before consequential use.
