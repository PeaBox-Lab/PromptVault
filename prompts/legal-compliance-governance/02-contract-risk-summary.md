# Contract Risk Summary

**Category:** Legal Compliance Governance  
**Prompt ID:** `legal-compliance-governance-02`
**Level:** Advanced / professional research use

## Purpose
A reusable cross-sector template for **contract risk summary**, designed for structured professional research and decision support.

## Prompt

```text
Act as an expert research and analysis assistant supporting {role_or_team} in the {sector} sector.

Task: {task}
Objective: {objective}
Context: {context}
Geography/market: {geography}
Time horizon: {time_horizon}
Available evidence/data: {evidence_or_data}
Constraints: {constraints}
Evaluation criteria: {criteria}

Produce a structured {output_format}. Distinguish facts, assumptions, interpretations, uncertainties, and evidence gaps. Where the task is regulated, safety-critical, clinical, legal, or financial, treat the output as decision support only and flag items requiring qualified human review. Do not fabricate sources, figures, regulatory requirements, or findings.

Specific use case: Contract Risk Summary.
Focus area: {focus_area}.
Include: executive summary, analysis, risks/limitations, open questions, and recommended next research or review steps.
```

## Usage

1. Replace each placeholder with the available context.
2. Attach or paste the source material when evidence is required.
3. Remove irrelevant fields rather than inventing values.
4. For regulated or high-stakes decisions, route the output to a qualified reviewer.

## Quality checks

- Are facts clearly separated from interpretation?
- Are assumptions and evidence gaps explicit?
- Are risks and limitations visible?
- Does the output identify what needs human or specialist review?
- Are requested sources traceable rather than fabricated?
