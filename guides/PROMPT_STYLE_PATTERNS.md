# Prompt Style Patterns

## Decision memo
Best for executives choosing among alternatives. Require options, weighted criteria, trade-offs, recommendation, conditions, and non-recommendations.

## Research protocol
Best for source-heavy work. Require questions, scope, source hierarchy, evidence register, contradiction handling, uncertainty, and gaps.

## Practitioner specification
Best for software, operations, HR, and product delivery. Require inputs, constraints, workflow, deliverables, acceptance criteria, risks, and definition of done.

## Red-team review
Best for challenging a plan or thesis. Ask for hidden assumptions, strongest counterarguments, failure modes, triggers, mitigations, and evidence that would reverse the conclusion.

## Extraction schema
Best for documents and datasets. Define fields, allowed values, null handling, confidence, evidence location, validation rules, and exact JSON output.

## Comparative matrix
Best for vendors, products, policies, investments, or technologies. Define alternatives, criteria, weights, scoring rules, evidence, sensitivities, and tie-breakers.

## Iterative copilot
Best for ambiguous or creative work. Require a brief, first draft, critique against a rubric, revision, and a final checklist.

## Tutor and assessor
Best for learning. Specify learner level, objective, misconceptions, worked examples, practice progression, feedback, and mastery test.
