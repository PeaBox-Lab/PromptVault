# Research Prompt Playbook

## Research workflow

1. Turn the business question into answerable sub-questions.
2. Establish scope, definitions, exclusions, geography, and date range.
3. Build a source hierarchy: primary, authoritative secondary, expert, and discovery-only sources.
4. Create an evidence table before writing conclusions.
5. Triangulate material claims with independent evidence where feasible.
6. Record contradictions and explain which evidence is more credible.
7. Separate facts, interpretation, assumptions, and recommendations.
8. Test conclusions against counterevidence and alternative explanations.
9. Quantify uncertainty and the decision impact of missing information.
10. Produce an evidence register and prioritized research gaps.

## Source hierarchy

- Tier 1: laws, regulations, standards, official filings, audited reports, government datasets, original research, contractual documents, and product documentation.
- Tier 2: reputable analyst research, peer-reviewed reviews, respected industry bodies, and established news organizations.
- Tier 3: expert commentary, conference material, vendor material, and practitioner publications.
- Discovery only: community posts, unverified aggregators, anonymous claims, and AI-generated summaries.

## Evidence record

Capture claim, source, publisher/author, date, URL/document reference, exact evidence, evidence tier, limitations, and confidence.

## Anti-hallucination instruction

`Do not invent sources, quotations, statistics, dates, people, organizations, URLs, or document contents. If a fact cannot be verified from the supplied or accessible evidence, label it Unverified and state how to verify it.`
